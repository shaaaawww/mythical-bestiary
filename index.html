About the Archive

This is a standalone, searchable reference bestiary. It contains 544 distinct beings assembled from a broad historical and folklore catalog, with modern fantasy and cryptid entries labeled separately.

Historical folklore identifies beings described in religious texts, folklore, oral traditions, art or historical bestiaries. Fantasy adaptation identifies later creative/game reinterpretations. Cryptid report identifies modern reports or legends, not scientifically verified animals.

Each entry includes a concise appearance summary, powers, broad cultural context, inferred habitat and ability categories, known limitations when present, related beings, and suggested areas for further reading. Some catalog categories represent classes of beings rather than individuals.

There is no single universal chronology for myths: many oral traditions cannot be given a reliable first date. Era filters indicate broad associations or surviving documentation only. For living Indigenous traditions, consult community-authored and nation-specific sources; archive generalizations are not definitive.

This edition contains no illustrations. Favorites save to this browser on this device if local storage is available.
